Title: Sensor malfunction produced false rising-temperature trend

Apparent temperature drift over several days was traced to a faulty temperature sensor reading progressively higher due to a loose connection, not an actual mechanical issue. Connection repaired, sensor recalibrated. Important reminder that not every sensor trend reflects a real mechanical trend — cross-check vibration alongside temperature before concluding a real issue is developing.
